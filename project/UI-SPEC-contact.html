01

Purpose & priority

The contact page is the 3rd build priority (after Homepage and Free Audit page). Its job: "gives people a simple way to reach out."

Two visitor intents land here, and the page serves both without forcing a choice:

IntentPathTreatment
"I want the free audit" (primary) Audit CTA → Free Audit page Primary — visually dominant, top-left
"I just have a question / want to talk fit" Inline message form Secondary — the form on this page

Do not rebuild the full audit form here. The audit lives on its own page; this page points to it.

5.5 Validation

  • Validate on blur per field; validate all on submit.
  • Email: standard email pattern → "Enter a valid email"
  • Required fields: Name, Business name, Email, field 6 → "This field is required"
  • Website: optional; normalize to https:// on submit if filled.
  • Phone: optional; accept free-form; do not over-validate.
  • Always pair error colour with visible text + aria-invalid="true" + aria-describedby pointing at the error node.
06

Success state

On successful submit, replace the form card's contents (keep the card) with a confirmation and the "What happens next" steps. Move keyboard focus to the heading.

  • Heading: "Message sent." — sans 30px, weight 500, small teal check badge (background:var(--teal50); color:var(--teal); border-radius:5px)
  • Confirmation line: "We will review your message and respond if there is a clear fit."
  • Focus management: tabindex="-1", role="status" / aria-live="polite" on the heading
  • Secondary actions: "← Send another message" (resets form) + persistent audit CTA "Get a Free Audit"

What happens next — 4 steps

01
We review your business
Site, local presence, Google profile, competitor position, obvious conversion gaps.
02
We identify the biggest opportunities
The changes most likely to improve visibility, trust, lead capture, and follow-up.
03
You receive the findings
A clear summary of where you stand and what we'd fix first.
04
If it makes sense, we talk next steps
A Growth Infrastructure Package or ongoing support.

Step numbers in --font-mono, --blue. Titles weight 600. Descriptions --mute, 14px.

Compliance & anti-spam

  • Honeypot field: visually hidden (position:absolute; left:-9999px), tabindex="-1", autocomplete="off", aria-hidden="true". Reject if filled.
  • Consent line (non-blocking, below submit): 12px, --mute2 — "By submitting, you agree to be contacted about your inquiry. We never share or sell your data."
⚠ NEEDS INPUT: If a marketing opt-in or hard privacy-policy checkbox is legally required, add a checkbox + link to /privacy. No privacy policy URL exists in the source material.

09

Accessibility

  • Every field has a real <label for>; placeholders are not a substitute for labels.
  • Logical DOM/focus order: hero → audit CTA → form fields top-to-bottom → submit.
  • Visible focus ring on all interactive elements (4px blue halo doubles as the focus indicator — also on buttons and links, not just inputs).
  • Color contrast: --ink on --paper passes AA; --mute (#6B635A) on paper passes AA for body text. --blue (#1F8FFF) on white is ~3:1 — acceptable for large text / UI accents, not for small body copy.
  • Errors announced via aria-live; success state focus-managed (§6).
  • Honeypot hidden with position:absolute; left:-9999px (not display:none) + aria-hidden="true".

Data handling & integrations

⚠ NEEDS INPUT: The source material specifies no backend. All items below are open decisions.
  • Submission target: CRM/form endpoint? Recommend a single POST that routes to the owner's inbox + CRM.
  • Fields → CRM mapping: field 6 (select) and Message are the qualifying signals; pass as structured fields, not just a blob.
  • Spam: honeypot is specced; add server-side rate limiting / optional CAPTCHA if abuse appears.
  • Notifications: owner email on every submit. Consider an autoresponder echoing the "What happens next" steps.
11

Open questions / gaps to resolve

01
No direct contact details exist — no email, phone, address, service area, hours, or social links anywhere in the copy doc. If any are real, add a compact "Other ways to reach us" block. Until then, the form + audit CTA are the only channels.
02
Response-time promise — the brand leans on "speed wins the lead." A concrete SLA (e.g. "We reply within 1 business day") would strengthen the page but isn't in the source. Confirm before stating a number.
03
Privacy policy URL — needed for the consent line / any required checkbox.
04
Button hover convention — cta-form.html hovers to --blue; the Brand Guide hovers to #000. Pick one site-wide. Recommend: solid buttons → #000, accent buttons → #0E7CEB.
05
Hero accent phrase — confirm whether to set a serif/blue accent in the headline (brand rhythm) or keep it all-sans.
