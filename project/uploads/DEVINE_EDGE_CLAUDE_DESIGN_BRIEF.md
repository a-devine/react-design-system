# Devine Edge — Claude Design Refinement File

## Purpose

Use this file in Claude Design to refine the visual identity, layout, and brand feel of the Devine Edge website.

The current site is strategically strong. The next design pass should focus on increasing:

- premium trust
- local business relevance
- visual clarity
- conversion confidence
- brand distinctiveness
- warmth
- polish

Do not redesign into a flashy AI startup site. The brand should feel like a high-trust growth infrastructure company for real local businesses.

---

# 1. Brand Context

Company:

> Devine Edge

Primary positioning:

> The operating system for modern local business growth.

Short positioning:

> AI-powered growth infrastructure for local businesses.

Core promise:

> We help local businesses become easier to find, easier to trust, and easier to choose.

Primary CTA:

> Get a Free AI Visibility Audit

Secondary CTA:

> Book a Strategy Call

---

# 2. Audience

The website is for local service business owners, especially:

- medical spas
- home service companies
- contractors
- dental and health practices
- legal and professional services
- high-ticket local service businesses

These visitors care about:

- calls
- bookings
- jobs
- appointments
- leads
- revenue
- trust
- reputation
- local visibility
- not falling behind competitors

They may be skeptical of agencies and confused by AI.

Design should make the value feel practical, credible, and modern.

---

Every page and section must communicate:

## Intelligence

The brand feels ahead of the market.

## Trust

The brand feels grounded, competent, and safe.

## Revenue Orientation

The brand feels focused on business outcomes, not vanity metrics.

# 6. Brand Identity Recommendations

## Brand Personality

Devine Edge should feel:

- sharp
- calm
- strategic
- premium
- practical
- local-business aware
- technically advanced but human

## Brand Should Not Feel

- neon AI
- cyberpunk
- overly dark
- generic agency
- guru-like
- cold SaaS
- fake enterprise
- cluttered

## Logo / Wordmark Direction

Recommended direction:

- clean wordmark
- strong typography
- minimal geometric edge mark
- subtle “edge” concept through angle, line, or directional geometry
- no robot, brain, spark, or generic AI icon

Potential mark ideas:

- refined angular edge symbol
- subtle forward slash / wedge
- geometric D/E monogram
- precision line mark
- local growth grid mark

## Color Direction

Use warm, premium neutrals.

Recommended palette direction:

- deep warm charcoal
- off-white / warm ivory
- graphite
- muted slate
- restrained teal, green, or bronze accent
- subtle low-saturation gradients

Avoid:

- electric blue / purple AI gradients
- neon green
- pure black cyber aesthetic
- rainbow gradient backgrounds
- overly corporate navy

## Typography Direction

Use a modern sans-serif with strong readability.

Headlines should feel:

- confident
- precise
- premium
- slightly tight but readable

Body should feel:

- calm
- clear
- accessible
- not overly technical

## Icon Direction

Icons should be:

- minimal
- line-based
- precise
- consistent stroke width
- not cartoonish
- not overly playful

# 7. Section Design Recommendations

## Hero

### Goal

Immediately communicate premium strategy and practical business value.

### Recommended Visuals

Use a system-style visual that shows:

- Google visibility
- AI search
- website
- ads
- automation
- revenue outcomes

Avoid:

- generic AI robot images
- abstract blobs without meaning
- too much dashboard clutter

### Hero Layout

Use a strong two-column desktop layout:

- left: headline, subheadline, CTA, microcopy
- right: system visual or layered infrastructure card

Mobile:

- headline first
- CTA visible quickly
- visual simplified

# 9. Mobile Design Rules

Mobile must be treated as a primary experience.

Rules:

- hero CTA visible without excessive scrolling
- cards stack cleanly
- no tiny text
- no horizontal scroll
- service cards shortened
- audit value card must be easy to read
- sticky CTA can be considered after scroll
- visual diagrams should simplify on mobile
