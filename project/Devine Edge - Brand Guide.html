02 — Logo & Mark

One arrow, always moving forward.

The mark is a geometric "E" cut into a forward arrow — edge and motion in a single glyph. Pair it with the wordmark for primary use; the mark stands alone only where the brand is already established.

Clear space

Keep a margin of x on all sides, where x equals the width of the arrow notch. Nothing — type, edges, or other marks — enters this zone.

Minimum sizes

  • Wordmark — never below 96 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print.
  • Mark — never below 20 px / 6 mm; favicon is the only exception.
  • Pairing — wordmark leads in all primary contexts; the standalone mark is for avatars, favicons, and app icons.
  • Color — ink on paper by default; reverse to paper on ink. Electric blue is reserved for the accent system, never the logo fill.

Do & don't

Use approved ink or reversed marks at full opacity.

Don't rotate, recolor with the accent, or skew the mark.

Don't stretch, condense, or alter the proportions.

Don't place on low-contrast or clashing color fields.

Warm paper, deep ink, one electric edge.

The serif accent

Use Instrument Serif italic — in electric blue — for at most one phrase per headline. It marks the emotional or human turn in a line. Never set body copy, UI, or whole headlines in it.

Setting rules

  • Headlines: tight tracking (-0.02 to -0.035em), line-height ~1.05.
  • Body: Mute on Paper, 1.5–1.6 line-height, ~62ch measure.
  • Labels & tags: Geist Mono, uppercase, 0.14–0.22em tracking.
05 — Voice & Tone

Talk like the sharpest operator in the room.

Confident, concrete, and generous with the truth. We sound like someone who has done this a hundred times and wants you to win — not a marketer working a funnel.

Specific over clever

Name the number, the page, the timeframe. "A 12-page audit in 48 hours" beats "actionable insights."

Plain over jargon

If a phrase needs a glossary, cut it. We translate the technical into the obvious.

Forward over passive

Every sentence points to the next step. Active verbs, short clauses, clear asks.

Radius

Cards 14px · wells 9px · buttons & fields fully rounded (999px).

Elevation

Soft, warm shadows only — a 1px tint line plus a long, low-opacity drop. Never hard or gray.

Texture

A faint dot grid sits behind paper surfaces at low opacity to add warmth without noise.

07 — Motion

Entrances that settle, never bounce around.

Motion is purposeful and brief. Elements arrive once, ease into place, and stay still. The forward arrow is our cue — things move ahead, then rest.

120–620 ms

Duration

Quick, layered entrances. Stagger related elements 60–120ms apart for rhythm.

easeOutBack

Easing

Decelerating curves with a faint overshoot on hero elements. Calm, not springy.

Once

On view

Entrances fire a single time after fonts load — never on every scroll. No loops.

Accessibility

Always honor prefers-reduced-motion. Users with the OS setting on see the final, settled state instantly — no entrance, no parallax, no delay on content.

08 — Imagery & Iconography

Real signal, drawn simply.

Imagery shows the product and the people who use it — never generic stock abstraction. Icons are thin, geometric line work that echoes the angles of the mark.

Icon style

Outlined, ~1.6px stroke, rounded joins. Match the optical weight of the surrounding text. No fills, no duotone, no emoji.

Photography

  • Warm & natural light — never cold or clinical.
  • Show the work and the win: real screens, real owners.
  • Crop with intent; leave room for the dot-grid texture to breathe.
  • Avoid stock clichés — handshakes, glowing AI brains, fake dashboards.
